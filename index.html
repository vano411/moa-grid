MOA Grid Target

Mark where each shot landed. The grid measures your group and tells you how far to dial.

Tap inside the grid to mark a shot. Use Undo last to take one back.

Off-grid shots

    Off-grid shots are saved with the group and included in exports. The grid can't measure them, so they are left out of group size.
